Our Story

From photographers.. to photographers

Our journey began from the heart of 'the location.' We faced difficulties in finding professional equipment at reasonable prices and with convenient conditions. From here, the idea of Gear Share was born; to be the first platform that combines the quality of equipment with the ease of procedures. We understand the value of every shot, and therefore we care about every detail in our equipment as if we ourselves were going to use it.

Our Values

Superior Quality: Every piece undergoes a rigorous performance and cleanliness check after each rental.
Technical Support: We are not just a rental company; our team of professional photographers is always ready to help you choose the lens or camera that best suits your project.
Transparency: No hidden fees. The listed prices are what you will actually pay, with a clear insurance policy that protects both parties.

Why Choose Us

Latest models: We constantly update our equipment list to keep up with the global camera market (Sony, Canon, Red, DJI).
Flexible scheduling: Pickup and delivery that fits your busy shooting schedule.
Creative community: By dealing with us, you join a large community of artists who share experiences and inspiration.
